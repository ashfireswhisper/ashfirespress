What the press is for

Some truths arrive before the category that can contain them.

Ashfires Press exists for work that does not fit neatly inside established forms. We publish books and papers that name what has been misnamed, preserve what has been dismissed, and make difficult experience legible without flattening it.

Books born from presence. Research built from lived reality.
